How to read this
  • Hot day: Tmax above the local 90th percentile for that time of year (1981-2010).
  • IMD heatwave: plains, Tmax at least 40 °C and at least 4.5 °C above normal, or at least 45 °C. Coastal (Mumbai, Ratnagiri), Tmax at least 37 °C and at least 4.5 °C above normal. Severe: departure of 6.5 °C or more, or Tmax of 47 °C or more on the plains.
  • Levels: Yellow when P(hot day) passes a threshold, Orange when P(heatwave) passes a threshold (both tuned on 2015-2018 for each model), Red when P(severe) is 50% or more.
  • Forecasts are the saved rolling backtest for 1 March to 30 June of 2015-2024. The Chronos models were used zero-shot, without training on Indian data.
